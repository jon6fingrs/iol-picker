Lens Implant Planner

Choosing a lens implant for cataract surgery

A few questions about what you want after surgery and about your eyes. Every kind of lens is sorted into good fits, possible with trade-offs, and usually not advised, with the reasons. It is meant to be filled in together with your surgeon or a member of their team, as a starting point for that conversation, not a decision.

Private by design. Everything entered stays on this device. Nothing is sent over the internet or saved, and Start over erases it.
